# Visualizing the Complex World of 1492

This is a project to create a general map of the human world in 1492. We call this the "Pivot of History," as it represents the height of a world on its own terms,
just prior to the imperial spread of European trade, rulers, religion, and political ideology, and is the tradition between the Medieval and Early Modern Eras.

Our ulterior motive is that it is a great opportunity to work on methods for representing political geography at a time when the nature of states, sovereignty,
and territoriality were much more complex and varied than today. We are experimenting with data models and analysis methods for visualizing this complexity.
